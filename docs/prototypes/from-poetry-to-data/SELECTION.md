# Prototype selection

The user selected an integrated hybrid on 2026-07-29:

- `A — narrative` is the default homepage direction. It introduces the project through the question of how Persian poetry changed across thirteen centuries.
- `B — explorer` defines the atlas entry and the primary search-and-filter exploration experience.
- `C — research` defines the research hub and research-result pages, with findings, evidence, methods, citations, and downloads presented together.
- `D — audience` supplies explicit entry paths for general readers, literary researchers, digital-humanities researchers, and data users.

All four directions are part of one information architecture, not four competing homepages. The audience paths guide visitors into the narrative, atlas, research, methodology, and data experiences appropriate to their primary task.

The prototype remains disposable. Do not copy its markup into production before the specification is approved.
